# Refunds and cancellations

This page explains how billing, refunds and cancellations work for every Hazelpad plan. It applies to personal and team workspaces alike.

## How billing works

Hazelpad plans are billed in advance. A monthly plan renews on the same day each month, and an annual plan renews once a year on the date you first subscribed. You can see your next renewal date under Settings, then Billing, and you can switch between monthly and annual billing from the same place. We send a reminder email seven days before an annual plan renews, so there is time to change or cancel it. Prices on the pricing page do not include local taxes, which are added at checkout based on your billing address.

## Refund windows

We want you to be happy with Hazelpad, and we know plans sometimes change after you pay. Annual plans can be refunded within 30 days of purchase. Monthly plans can be refunded within 7 days of purchase. After the refund window has closed we cannot refund the current billing period, but you can still cancel so that the plan does not renew. Refunds go back to the original payment method and usually arrive within five to ten business days, depending on your bank.

## How to ask for a refund

Open Settings, choose Billing, and select Request a refund next to the payment you want returned. Tell us briefly why you are leaving, which helps us improve. You will get a confirmation email straight away and a second email when the refund has been sent. If you paid through the Apple App Store or Google Play, the refund has to be requested from that store, because they hold the payment.

## Cancelling without a refund

You can cancel at any time. When you cancel, your plan stays active until the end of the period you have paid for, and then your workspace moves to the free plan. Nothing is deleted when that happens. Notes above the free plan limit become read-only until you upgrade again or remove some.

## Team plans

On a team plan, only the workspace owner can request a refund or cancel. Seats added part way through a billing period are charged for the remaining time only. If you remove a seat, the unused time is kept as account credit and used against your next invoice, and it is not paid out as a refund.

## Exceptions

If you were charged twice, or charged after cancelling, write to support and we will correct it whatever the date. Where local consumer law gives you a longer refund period than ours, the longer period applies.
